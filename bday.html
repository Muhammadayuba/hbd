Happy Birthday Ummi

Winter whispers,
Her call to a Hazel,
A witch hazel,
Risen with warmth,
Resilient as frost,
A pure abode,
A symbol of goodness,
The mother of virtue —
As she's called.

Gather all
To exult in this joyous day,
A birth of purity,
Relived and remembered.

I choose to celebrate
Her existence,
In poetry and rejoice,
As she's completed another chapter,
While I wait and pray
For a life both easy
And successful to unfold.
By M.Ayuba
